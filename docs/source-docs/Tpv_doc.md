**Third-Party Vendor (TPV) – Complete Implementation Flow**

**1. Third-Party Vendor Registration**

During Third-Party Vendor registration, the vendor can select one of two vendor types:

* Temporary Third-Party Vendor
* Permanent Third-Party Vendor

The vendor enters the required company/vendor information and submits the registration. After submission, the vendor waits for Admin approval.

**Next Step:** Pending Admin Approval → Admin Vendor Activation

**2. Admin Approval / Vendor Activation**

The Admin logs into the system and reviews the registered Third-Party Vendor details. After verification, the Admin approves/activates the vendor.

Once the vendor is activated, the system automatically sends an activation email to the vendor.

**Next Step:** Vendor Activation → Vendor Login

**3. Activation / Credential Email**

After the Admin activates the vendor, an automatic activation email is sent to the Third-Party Vendor.

The email provides the information required for the vendor to access the Third-Party Vendor Portal.

**Next Step:** Vendor Login → Kickoff Meeting

**4. Kickoff Meeting**

After the vendor is activated, the Admin creates a Kickoff Meeting for the Third-Party Vendor.

While creating the Kickoff Meeting, all required meeting information is captured, including the meeting date/time, participants, discussion points, requirements, remarks, and other applicable details.

**Main Activities:**

* Create Kickoff Meeting
* Select Third-Party Vendor
* Add participants
* Set meeting date/time
* Add required discussion points
* Capture meeting details/remarks
* Complete the meeting

**Next Step:** Kickoff Meeting Completed → MOM/PDF Generation

**5. Kickoff Meeting MOM / PDF**

Once the Kickoff Meeting is completed, the finalized meeting information is used to generate the Kickoff Meeting MOM/PDF.

The generated PDF is shared with the relevant participants and the Third-Party Vendor.

**Purpose:**
To create an official record of the finalized Kickoff Meeting discussion, requirements, and decisions.

**Next Step:** MOM/PDF Generated → Vendor Onboarding

**6. Third-Party Vendor Onboarding – 6 Steps**

After the Kickoff Meeting, the Third-Party Vendor proceeds with the main onboarding process.

The vendor onboarding process contains the following six business steps:

**Step 1 – Kickoff Meeting**

The vendor reviews the finalized Kickoff Meeting MOM/PDF and completes the required acknowledgement.

**Purpose:**
To confirm that the vendor has reviewed and acknowledged the finalized Kickoff Meeting information.

**Step 2 – Company Details**

The vendor provides and completes the required company and business information.

**Purpose:**
To capture complete and accurate company information required for vendor onboarding and verification.

**Step 3 – Document Submission**

The vendor uploads and submits all required company/vendor documents.

**Purpose:**
To collect the necessary documents required for vendor verification, compliance, and onboarding.

**Step 4 – Under Review**

The submitted company information and documents are placed under Admin review and verification.

**Purpose:**
To allow the Admin to review and verify the information and documents submitted by the vendor.

**Step 5 – Confirmation**

After the required review and verification process is completed, the vendor receives onboarding confirmation.

**Purpose:**
To confirm that the vendor's onboarding information and requirements have been reviewed and confirmed.

**Step 6 – Start Work / Welcome Letter**

After onboarding approval, the applicable Start Work Letter / Welcome Letter process is completed.

**Purpose:**
To officially confirm that the vendor has successfully completed the onboarding process and can proceed with the next stage of work.

**7. Final Admin Approval**

After the vendor completes all required onboarding steps, the Admin performs the final review.

The Admin verifies the submitted information and approves the completed onboarding.

**Purpose:**
To officially complete and approve the Third-Party Vendor onboarding process.

**Next Step:** Admin Approval → Welcome Email

**8. Welcome Email**

After the Admin approves the vendor onboarding, the system automatically sends a Welcome Email to the Third-Party Vendor.

The email confirms that the vendor's onboarding has been successfully completed and approved.

**Next Step:** Welcome Email → Workforce Eligibility

**9. Workforce Eligibility**

Once the Third-Party Vendor onboarding is successfully completed and approved by the Admin, the vendor becomes eligible to add Workforce members.

The vendor can then add the required Workforce members for their organization, project, or site.

**Next Step:** Workforce Eligible → Add Workforce

**10. Add Workforce**

An eligible Third-Party Vendor can add a new Workforce member through the vendor portal.

The required personal, professional, and compliance-related information of the Workforce member is captured in the system.

**Next Step:** Workforce Created → Workforce Onboarding

**11. Workforce Onboarding**

After a Workforce member is added, the Workforce onboarding process begins.

The Workforce member must complete the applicable onboarding and compliance requirements, including:

* Medical
* Induction
* PPE
* Entry Badge

Each required step is completed before the Workforce member proceeds to the next stage.

**12. Workforce – Medical**

The required Medical process is completed for the Workforce member.

Medical-related information, documents, or verification details are recorded in the system.

**Purpose:**
To verify that the Workforce member meets the required medical eligibility criteria.

**13. Workforce – Induction**

The Workforce member completes the required Induction process.

This may include applicable safety, site, process, and organization-related requirements.

**Purpose:**
To formally induct the Workforce member and ensure they understand the applicable site and process requirements.

**14. Workforce – PPE**

The Workforce member completes the required PPE process.

PPE issuance, information, or acknowledgement is recorded in the system as applicable.

**Purpose:**
To ensure that the Workforce member meets the required Personal Protective Equipment compliance requirements.

**15. Workforce – Entry Badge**

The Workforce member completes the Entry Badge process.

Badge-related information and applicable site-entry requirements are completed and recorded in the system.

**Purpose:**
To complete the required authorization and badge process for site access.

The complete TPV implementation provides a structured lifecycle for managing Third-Party Vendors from initial registration through vendor approval, Kickoff Meeting, vendor onboarding, final approval, and Workforce onboarding.
