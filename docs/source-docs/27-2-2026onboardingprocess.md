**Admin Side**

The registration process will be done from the Admin Panel or Thirdparty Vendor Registration Page.

If the Third Party Vendor is already registered in the system, the admin can directly proceed with the existing vendor.

During the kickoff meeting process

In this section, there will be a dropdown to select the Third Party Vendor.

If no vendor exists in the list, a plus (+) option will be available to add a new Third Party Vendor.

The admin can add the new vendor details through this option.

Once the vendor is added, there will be a button to send login credentials to the Third Party Vendor.

The Vendor Code will be auto-generated by the system.

After that admin can view the vendor list and select workers for the project.

The system will also track who entered or assigned the workers.

A Kickoff Meeting form will be created with fields such as date, time, venue, meeting mode (online/offline), participants, organization, designation, role, target date, and responsible person.

The system will auto-prefill client and related details.

After submitting the form, a prefilled Kickoff Meeting document will be generated and stored.

The PDF will also be shared with vendors and workers through email or WhatsApp.

**Third Party Vendor**

After receiving the document, the vendor will log in and the first step will display the Kickoff Meeting details. The onboarding process will include:

Step 1: KOM

Step 2: Vendor Profile and details

Step 3: Legal Documents

Step 4: Approval

Step 5: Confirmation and after confirmation Start arrow notification or button by clicking that open workforce tab

The Workforce module will begin after confirmation. The process will include:

Step 1: Team Addition (Workforce onboarding tab)

Step 2: Medical

Step 3: Induction

Step 4: PPE (this step can be skipped if required)

Step 5: Card Status
