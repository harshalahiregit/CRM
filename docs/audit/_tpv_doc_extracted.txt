
Sangoe TPV — Recommended Complete Module
Third-Party Vendor, Contractor, Workforce & Risk Management System
1. Purpose
Sangoe TPV is a complete lifecycle management system for managing third-party vendors, contractors, subcontractors, service providers and their deployed workforce across projects, sites and operational environments.
TPV should not be treated as a simple Vendor Master or Vendor CRUD module.
It should manage the complete lifecycle:
Vendor Identification → Prequalification → Risk Assessment → Approval → Contract → Onboarding → Mobilisation → Workforce Deployment → Training & Competency → PPE → Work Authorization → Gate Access → Operational Monitoring → Meetings → Compliance → Inspection → Incident/NCR → CAPA → Performance → Renewal/Extension → Offboarding
The objective is to provide management with a single source of truth for:
Who the third party is
What work they are authorized to perform
Where they are authorized to work
Which workers are deployed
Whether those workers are competent and compliant
Whether required PPE and permits are available
Whether the vendor is meeting contractual and HSE requirements
What issues/actions/decisions are outstanding
How the vendor is performing
Whether the vendor should continue, be suspended, renewed or exited

36. Critical Business Rules
The TPV engine should enforce rules rather than merely display information.
Rule 1 — No Approval, No Activation
A vendor cannot become Active until mandatory approvals are complete.
Rule 2 — No Mandatory Compliance, No Mobilisation
Mandatory onboarding requirements must be completed before mobilisation.
Rule 3 — No Valid Worker Compliance, No Access
A worker with expired mandatory requirements cannot receive/retain active access.
Rule 4 — No Competency, No Work Authorization
A worker cannot be authorized for activities for which competency is missing or expired.
Rule 5 — No Required PPE, No Work
Mandatory PPE requirements must be satisfied before applicable work authorization.
Rule 6 — No Permit, No High-Risk Work
Applicable high-risk work cannot commence without a valid permit.
Rule 7 — Temporary Means Temporary
Temporary vendor/workforce/access records automatically expire unless extended through approval.
Rule 8 — Expiry Drives Risk
Document, contract, training, medical, permit and certification expiry should automatically change compliance status.
Rule 9 — Repeated Violations Escalate
Strike rules automatically trigger escalation according to configured thresholds.
Rule 10 — Performance Influences Renewal
Vendor renewal should consider historical performance, compliance and risk.
Rule 11 — Every Action Has an Owner
MOM, audit, NCR, CAPA and inspection actions cannot remain ownerless.
Rule 12 — Every Closure Requires Evidence Where Configured
Closure should require supporting evidence for defined action types.

40. Sangoe TPV Positioning
The module should ultimately be positioned internally as:
Sangoe TPV — Third-Party Vendor, Contractor & Workforce Governance
Not merely:
Vendor Management
Because its scope is much broader.
The system combines:
Vendor Management + Contractor Management + Workforce Management + HSE Compliance + Control of Work + Access Control + Meeting/MOM + Performance Management + Risk Management + Offboarding
This lifecycle approach is consistent with modern contractor-management architectures, which increasingly connect prequalification, onboarding, operational delivery, access/control, compliance, performance, renewal and exit rather than treating these as isolated processes. (Vedo Group)

The key principle
TPV should not ask only: "Who is our vendor?"
It should allow Sangoe to answer, at any moment:
Who is the vendor?What are they contracted to do?Where are they working?Who are their people?Are those people qualified and compliant?Are they authorized to perform this work?Who is currently inside the site?What meetings, decisions and actions are outstanding?What incidents/NCRs/CAPAs exist?How is the vendor performing?What is their current risk?Should we continue, improve, renew, suspend or exit them?
That is the level at which I would build Sangoe TPV v1. It also leaves a clean architectural path for the AI/risk-intelligence layer without having to redesign the core module later.
