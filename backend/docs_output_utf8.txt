HR & Recruitment CRM Module - Requirement Specification
Project: Recruitment CRM ModuleSource: Meeting recording requirement analysis
Overview
This document summarizes the functional requirements discussed in the meeting for the Recruitment CRM module.
1. Dashboard
Dashboard should display Manpower Requests, Job Postings, Candidates, Interviews, Offer Letters, Onboarding and Employee statistics.
2. Manpower Request
Internal request creation, department/job/project/salary/level/JD summary, requester tracking, L1 Department Head approval, L2 Management approval before HR execution.
3. Job Posting
Convert approved manpower request into Job Description, edit missing details, AI JD improvement, publish to TrulyTalent and other portals, maintain posting status.
4. Candidate
Fetch applicants from TrulyTalent, AI resume screening, JD vs Resume matching score, mandatory/custom questions, candidate stages.
5. Interview
Support HR/L1/L2/Client/Vendor/Third Party interviewers, Google Meet link generation, Email & WhatsApp notifications, reminders, interview comments and history.
6. Onboarding
Selected candidates complete onboarding forms before offer letter generation, document verification, onboarding status tracking.
7. Offer Letter
Generate PDF offer letter from template after onboarding approval, auto-fill candidate details, email delivery.
8. Candidate Journey
Track complete journey from request → approval → job posting → application → interview → onboarding → offer letter → employee.
9. AI Features
AI JD enhancement, AI resume score, AI recommendation, AI question analysis.
10. Future Enhancements
Analytics, reports, notification center, audit logs, integrations.
Business Rules
L1 approval by Department Head.
L2 approval by Management.
HR can post only after approvals.
Job Posting created from approved request.
Interview notifications through Email, WhatsApp and Calendar.
Offer Letter only after onboarding completion.
Every candidate maintains full workflow history.
