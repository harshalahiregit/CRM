Product Requirement Document (PRD) 
HR Recruitment Module (Phase 1) 
Product Name 
Recruitment Management System (RMS) 
Version 
V1.0 
Objective 
To build a simple recruitment management system that helps HR teams manage the hiring 
process efficiently with one AI-powered Resume Screening Agent while keeping the 
remaining processes manual. 
Problem Statement 
Current recruitment processes are managed using Excel sheets, emails, and multiple 
portals, resulting in delayed hiring, manual screening effort, scattered candidate data, lack 
of recruitment tracking, and increased time-to-hire. 
Proposed Solution 
Develop an HR Recruitment Module that manages job requisitions, stores candidate 
information, uses one AI agent for resume screening, tracks interviews and offers, and 
manages onboarding activities. 
Scope 
In Scope: 
• Job Requisition Management 
• Job Posting 
• Candidate Management 
• Resume Screening Agent (AI) 
• Interview Management 
• Offer Management 
• Onboarding 
User Roles 
HR Executive: Create jobs, manage candidates, schedule interviews, generate offers. 
Hiring Manager: Approve requisitions, review shortlisted candidates, submit interview 
feedback. 
Admin: Manage configurations, reports, users, and permissions.

Recruitment Workflow 
1. Hiring Request Raised 
2. Manager Approval 
3. Job Posting 
4. Candidate Applications Received 
5. AI Resume Screening 
6. HR Reviews Shortlisted Candidates 
7. Interview Scheduling 
8. Interview Feedback 
9. Final Selection 
10. Offer Letter 
11. Document Collection 
12. Employee Joining
