## 🎯 Presentation Tips:

### **Timing:**
- Title & Intro: 2 mins
- Problem & Solution: 3 mins
- Technology: 2 mins
- Feature Demo: 15 mins (MAIN PART)
- Highlights & Metrics: 3 mins
- Status & Future: 3 mins
- Q&A: 5 mins
**Total: 30-35 minutes**

### **During Demo:**
1. **Start with Dashboard** - show overview
2. **Go to AI Feature FIRST** - most impressive
3. **Walk through complete flow** - requisition to employee
4. **Show automation** - emails, Meet links
5. **End with Dashboard again** - show updated stats

### **Key Phrases to Use:**
- "हे AI-powered आहे" (emphasize AI)
- "Automatic होतं" (emphasize automation)
- "80% time saved" (concrete numbers)
- "Production ready" (show confidence)
- "Scalable architecture" (technical credibility)
